# Scientific and ethical boundaries

PhiCloneLab is a **conceptual teaching and evidence-tracing tool**, not an experimental design system.

## Evidence classes

- **Validated in animals:** A historical animal research demonstration. This does not imply an equivalent human procedure is established.
- **Established cell science:** Evidence about cellular processes; does not demonstrate reproduction.
- **Proposal / unverified:** An expressed plan, hypothesis, correspondence, or claimed research direction. Not a validated outcome.
- **Ethics:** Professional guidance and boundary-setting, not an experimental result.

## Modeling rules

1. Preserve distinct concepts: nuclear genome, mitochondrial DNA, cell identity, epigenetics, developmental context, and personal identity.
2. Never calculate an experimental success rate from arbitrary sliders; qualitative lenses only modify narrative interpretation.
3. Never claim that cloning replicates memory, experience, consciousness, or identity.
4. Link source summaries to original documents or established institutional reference material.
5. Never translate this educational material into practical instructions for human reproductive cloning.

## Limitations

The stages shown in the app are **concept categories**, not chronological wet-lab instructions. The static evidence ledger is a curated starter set; entries should be critically reviewed and periodically checked for broken links or scientific updates.

## Next milestones

Versioned source provenance, study-level metadata, reproducible animal-data visualizations with uncertainty intervals, offline reading packs, and accessible SVG relationship graphs. Any future numerical model must specify published datasets, calibration assumptions, and independent validation.
